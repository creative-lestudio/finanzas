Frequently asked questions

Before you install it.

Does it connect to my bank?

No, and that's on purpose. We never ask for your bank username or password and we can't see your accounts. You log what you spend, or a shortcut logs it for you, and it stays encrypted.

Can I log an expense without opening the app?

Yes. On iPhone, with the Shortcuts app you can open “New transaction” pre-filled when you pay with Apple Pay, or with two taps on the back of the phone. The instructions are in Settings. You can also dictate it by voice.

Can I scan a receipt?

Yes. You take a photo and the AI fills in the amount, merchant, date and category. The photo is only used to read it: it isn't saved on your device or on our servers.

Does it work in my country?

Yes: in Spain, Venezuela, the United States and the rest of the world. At the start you choose your region and accounts, currency and lessons for your country are set up. Each account can have its own currency and, if you're paid or pay in another, the app stores the exact equivalent. It's available in Spanish and English.

Is it free?

Yes, for now it's free and ad-free. If we add a paid plan in the future, what you already use will remain yours and you'll be able to export it.

Is my data safe?

It's encrypted on your device with AES-256 and a key derived from your password. If you sync, only already-encrypted data reaches the server and each account only accesses its own.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use it only on your device, offline. If you want your data on several devices, sign in with Google or your email.

How do the bolivar rates work?

The app downloads the official rates (BCV dollar and euro) and the parallel one every day; you can also enter your own. When logging a payment you choose the rate or the agreed amount, and the app counts it in your main currency.

Why does the APK ask for permission to install?

Because you download it from this website and not from Google Play. Android always asks in that case; you can remove the permission after installing.

Can I sign in with Face ID or fingerprint?

Yes. The first time you type your encryption key; then the app offers to turn on Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or Windows Hello, and you sign in without typing it. The key is stored only on your device.

What about iPhone?

On iPhone it's installed from Safari with “Add to Home Screen”: it gets its icon and opens full screen, like an app.
